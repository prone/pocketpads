## App name

PocketPads: Sample Sequencer

## Short description

64 pads, a step sequencer and effects. Make beats and whole songs, offline.

## Full description

PocketPads is a pad sampler and step sequencer for making beats and finishing songs. It was built for handheld game consoles, so it works with physical buttons and sticks as well as by touch, and it needs no account and no internet connection.

MAKE A BEAT
• 64 pads in four banks of 16. Tap them, or play them from the buttons.
• A step sequencer with eight patterns of up to 128 steps each. Draw hits in, accent them, and give any step its own note to write bass lines and melodies.
• Record your playing into the pattern as it loops. Note repeat and swing are built in.

USE ANY SOUND
• Start with a built-in kit: a starter kit, or ambient, techno, house and hip hop kits. Every sound is made by the app, so there is nothing to download.
• Load your own WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC or OGG files.
• Record with the microphone and have the take chopped across the pads.
• Chop a loop into slices by ear, or let the app find the hits.
• Shape each pad: volume, pitch, fine tune, start and end, fades, filter, time stretch, bit crush, reverse, and chords from a single note.

BUILD A SONG
• Chain patterns into a full arrangement and watch it play through.
• Each style comes with four ready-made songs to take apart and change, or start with just the sounds.
• Export the song as a WAV file.

PERFORM
• Hold a button for stutter, tape stop or filter sweeps on the whole mix.
• Delay, reverb and a compressor on the master.
• Resample what you hear onto a pad.

CONTROLLERS
• Works with built-in gamepad controls on Android handhelds.
• Bluetooth MIDI pad controllers play the pads, and their knobs can be assigned to sound settings.
• On a phone or tablet, on-screen buttons appear on first touch.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
PocketPads collects no data and has no ads. The app does not have permission to use the internet. Your projects and recordings stay on your device.

OPEN SOURCE
PocketPads is free and open source under the MIT licence. The code and a full guide are at github.com/prone/pocketpads.

The Android version is new. If something does not work on your device, please report it on the project's issue tracker.
